Email signups

If you sign up for the weekly email list, your email address is collected and stored via Formspree, a third-party form service. We use it only to send the weekly digest and do not sell or share it. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in each email.

Event listings

Event data shown on this site is pulled from public sources — regional library systems and municipal Parks & Recreation calendars in BC. We don't control or verify third-party listings' accuracy; always confirm details on the source organization's own site before attending.
