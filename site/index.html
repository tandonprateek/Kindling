Frequently asked questions

Is Sproutlist free?

Yes. Browsing the site and the weekly email are both free. Some individual events may have their own admission cost — that's shown on each listing.

Where do the events come from?

Directly from public sources: regional library systems and municipal Parks & Recreation departments across BC. Listings refresh daily.

Which parts of BC does Sproutlist cover?

Lower Mainland, Fraser Valley, and Vancouver Island are live now, with Okanagan/Interior and Northern BC coverage being added.

How often is the list updated?

The site refreshes daily, and the email digest goes out once a week, every Friday.
